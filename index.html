“I like things that are loud and beautiful…” Chester Bennington, Billboard interview, May 2017
  • Born: March 20, 1976, Phoenix, Arizona.
  • Voice of Linkin Park: from 1999 to 2017.
  • Other projects: Grey Daze, Dead by Sunrise and Stone Temple Pilots.
  • Passed away: July 20, 2017.
  • His legacy: he spoke openly about his struggles with depression and addiction, and his music helped many people feel less alone.
